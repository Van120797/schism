Schism Atlas

Every event carries two prices: Belief — the YES odds on Polymarket, with the matching Kalshi market shown beside it wherever Kalshi runs the same question — and Price — what the linked instrument implies. The gap is the distance between the odds and the fair probability implied by the perp's price, volatility and time left. The Gap Score (0–100) blends gap size with a backtest over the last 7 days: how often gaps like this one closed, and how fast. The Convergence Clock is that backtested pace, applied to today's gap.

Rows start from verified snapshots — Polymarket odds observed Nov 9, 2026, 18:29 UTC, Kalshi odds 19:11 UTC, Robinhood Stock Token prices 19:13 UTC — and flip to Live as fresh data lands. Flow — Robinhood Chain (4663)

Real on-chain flow for the Stock Tokens behind the stock events: every Uniswap pool swap on Robinhood Chain in the last 24 hours, aggregated by DexScreener's chain index. Net pressure is buys minus sells; the flow z-score tests the buy share against 50/50. Large individual transfers and named wallets need the Blockscout API, which currently answers programmatic reads with 403 (bot protection) — so this panel shows pool flow only, and says so, rather than inventing wallet data. Crypto events (BTC, ETH) have no Stock Token, so their Flow stays the Coinbase volume z-score, labelled as a proxy wherever it appears.

Proof & method

No numbers here are invented. Every figure traces to a public source, and every model choice is written down.

Belief — Polymarket + Kalshi

YES odds, order-book context and 7-day odds history come from Polymarket's public APIs, refreshed live in your browser. Kalshi is mapped market-by-market: five Atlas events have a Kalshi market asking the same (or nearest) question, and their odds sit beside Polymarket's in every row — refreshed live through this site's own serverless proxy where it's deployed.

  • gamma-api.polymarket.com/events + clob.polymarket.com/prices-history — Polymarket, live
  • /.netlify/functions/kalshi — this site's same-origin serverless proxy, calling api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/markets/{ticker} (Kalshi Trade API v2) server-side. Mapped tickers: KXBTCMAXY-26DEC31-99999.99, KXBTCMINMON-BTC-26NOV30-7000000, KXETHMAXMON-ETH-26NOV30-300000, KXETHMINMON-ETH-26NOV30-200000, KXINXMAXY-01JAN2027-7999.99 (nearest equivalent — Kalshi asks for a touch of 7,999.99 by Jan 1; Polymarket asks for a December close above 8,000)
  • Kalshi YES = midpoint of best bid/ask. Kalshi's API returns 403 to any request carrying a browser Origin header and sends no CORS headers (verified Nov 10, 2026), so a browser can never read it directly — the proxy reads it server-side, where no Origin is sent, which is why Kalshi can be live here at all. Where the proxy function isn't deployed (a static mirror or an offline copy), the page falls back to trying Kalshi directly and then to the verified snapshot, observed Nov 9, 2026, 19:11 UTC and labelled as a snapshot. Nothing is estimated in Kalshi's name.
  • Single-name stocks (TSLA, AAPL, NVDA, COIN, MSTR, SPY) have no Kalshi price-threshold market — Kalshi's stock series track deliveries and KPIs, not prices — so those rows honestly show “No Kalshi equivalent”.
  • Each Atlas row links to its market on polymarket.com; Kalshi markets are on kalshi.com

Price — Robinhood Stock Tokens

Stock and ETF prices are the actual Robinhood Stock Tokens trading on Robinhood Chain (4663) — ERC-20s issued as “{Ticker} • Robinhood Token”, priced from their deepest Uniswap pool on the chain via DexScreener's index. Every address below was verified on-chain with symbol() / name() calls to the public RPC before use. Crypto prices still stream from Coinbase Exchange.

  • api.dexscreener.com/latest/dex/tokens/{address} — chain robinhood, deepest-liquidity pool sets the price; snapshot observed Nov 9, 2026, 19:13 UTC
  • rpc.mainnet.chain.robinhood.com — chain ID 4663 verified (eth_chainId = 0x1237); the Flow panel reads live block height from it
  • One exception, labelled in its row: the S&P 500 index event (SPX) has no Stock Token — its price stays the Yahoo Finance index quote, a proxy. The 7-day price history behind the chart is still the v1 snapshot series.
  • Fair value: a lognormal model — realized volatility from the 7-day price history, time to resolution, strike vs spot. An estimate, never a quote.

Flow — on-chain money

Flow is the tie-breaker layer, and for stock events it is now real Robinhood Chain money: all Uniswap pool swaps in the event's Stock Token over the last 24 hours, summed across the pairs DexScreener indexes for chain 4663 (up to 30 pairs per token). Net pressure and the buy-share z-score are computed from those counts — flow-led signals only fire on measured swaps (|z| ≥ 2 and net imbalance ≥ 2% of trades).

  • api.dexscreener.com/latest/dex/tokens/{address} — txns h24 buys/sells + volume h24 per pool, aggregated per token; snapshot Nov 9, 2026, 19:13 UTC, live on refresh
  • Intended next source: robinhoodchain.blockscout.com/api/v2 (Blockscout API v2 — token transfers, large transfers, notable wallets). It currently returns 403 to programmatic reads, so no wallet-level claims are made until it opens up.
  • BTC / ETH events have no Stock Token: their Flow remains the Coinbase perp-volume z-score, labelled “Coinbase proxy” in the Radar.

How the scores work

  • Gap = YES odds − model fair value, in percentage points. Positive: belief runs hotter than price.
  • Gap Score = 60% gap size (capped at 50 pts) + 40% backtested close rate.
  • Backtest: over the loaded 7 days, every hour the gap stood at ≥ 8 pts, did it halve within 48 hours? That fraction is the close rate; the median halving time drives the Convergence Clock.
  • Radar: a layer leads when its 1-hour move exceeds 2σ of its prior 30 one-hour moves. Flow leads when the Stock Token's 24h buy share breaks 2σ from 50/50 with at least 2% net imbalance.
  • Venue spread = Polymarket YES − Kalshi YES midpoint, shown where a Kalshi market is mapped. Two venues disagreeing is a schism of its own — it is displayed, never averaged away.
  • Global leaderboard: a Call the Close call resolves when its event resolves or the gap has halved (the backtest's close definition); the result is then posted to /.netlify/functions/leaderboard and stored in Netlify Blobs. It is a community board with no login — anyone can post under any display name, so it is not Sybil-proof and proves nothing by itself. The local board on your device is unaffected by it and stays yours.
  • Snapshot rule: anything not refreshed live keeps its Snapshot badge and its observation time. Stale is labelled, never hidden.
